Everything in one place

Built for the workday, not the paperwork

Four modules that cover the HR tasks your team actually does every day.

Attendance

One-tap check-in and check-out, with a clean history of hours worked, late days and absences.

Leave Management

Employees apply in seconds, balances update automatically, and managers approve from a single queue.

Payroll

Transparent payslips with a full breakdown of basic pay, allowances and deductions, every month.

Employee Profiles

A single source of truth for contact details, department, designation and reporting lines.

Admin Console

HR gets a real-time view of who's in, who's out, and what's waiting on their approval.

Role-based Access

Employees see their own records; admins see the whole organization. Nothing gets crossed.
